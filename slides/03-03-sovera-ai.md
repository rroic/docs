# Content

content-heading: States, orchestrator, tools - all in one Agent platform
content-body: Sovera orchestrates the Intelligence layer. It handles reasoning, states, multimodels, HITL, blueprints, MCPs and much more.  

In this case we used it our AI transformation. It runs twelve specialised agents, from knowledge ingestion to AI-detection evaluators, all defined as YAML blueprints. No ad-hoc code.

bullet-1-title: Blueprints in Sovera to evaluate our own transformation
bullet-1-body: We set KPIs on upskilling for our team accross the board. We build agents to evaluate and certify the new skills.

bullet-2-title: The quizes also serve as mentoring
bullet-2-body: Our certification teaches people where they might be lacking in upskilling. When they retake the test, the test focuses mostly on the parts "failed" in the previous attempt

bullet-3-title: Evaluation was designed in
bullet-3-body: The upskilling system has dedicated evaluation agents — behavioral anomaly detection, AI-generation detection, exercise scoring — built into the architecture from day one. 

bullet-4-title: Orchestration, not monolith
bullet-4-body: Competency assessment combines self-assessment, manager observations, and peer feedback across seven skill dimensions. Three input lenses, one coordinator agent, majority-based scoring.
