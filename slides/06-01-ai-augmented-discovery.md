# Bullets

bullet-1-title: The question has shifted

bullet-1-body: It is no longer "what should we build?" for our clients — it is "what should we build next?" for them. AI lets us discover faster what our clients competitors are doing, what their users actually want, and whether we are planning to address that in the right way.



bullet-2-title: More research, less overhead

bullet-2-body: AI assists with user interview synthesis, affinity mapping, persona drafting, and insight clustering — tasks that used to eat days. The research stays human. The admin shrinks.



bullet-3-title: Prototype in the room, not a week later

bullet-3-body: Tools like Lovable and Figma Make let us go from idea to testable prototype during a discovery session. Clients react to something real. Feedback loops compress from weeks to hours.



bullet-4-title: Backlog and specs as code

bullet-4-body: Claude Code for backlog generation, specs-as-code, architecture diagrams on demand. Discovery outputs that used to take a week to document can be ready before the client debrief.



bullet-5-title: No fixed recipe — and that is the point

bullet-5-body: Best practices are changing all the time. What works is curiosity, experimentation, and willingness to try new tools and techniques as they emerge. The more we treat discovery as an evolving craft, the better results we unlock for our clients — and the harder it is for their competition to keep up.
