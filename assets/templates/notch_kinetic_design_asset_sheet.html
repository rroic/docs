# Notch Kinetic Design Asset Sheet To help you recreate these designs in PowerPoint or any other presentation tool, here are the core design tokens and assets. ## 1. Typography * **Primary Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Available for free on Google Fonts) * **Secondary/Body Font:** Manrope (Available for free on Google Fonts) ## 2. Color Palette * **Primary Mint (Headlines):** `#2CC295` * **Deep Corporate (Backgrounds/Text):** `#0E1D26` * **Soft Accent (Containers):** `#F5FAFF` * **Subtle Neutral (Labels/Borders):** `#BBCAC1` * **Success/Action Green:** `#1A7B5F` ## 3. Image Assets I have generated high-quality, architectural imagery for your slides. You can download the individual images I've used by looking at the placeholders on the canvas. ## 4. Design Principles * **Structural Asymmetry:** Use off-center layouts and overlapping elements to create a dynamic, modern feel. * **Intentional Whitespace:** Don't crowd the slides. Use the "Notch" aesthetic by leaving plenty of room for elements to breathe. * **Vertical Accents:** Use thin vertical lines (matching the Mint green) to separate sections or highlight key data points. --- *Use these values to set up your PowerPoint Slide Master for a consistent look across all presentations.*
