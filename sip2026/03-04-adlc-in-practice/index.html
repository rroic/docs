Agents Development Life Cycle

The map, applied. Candid.

architecture

CV tailoring that actually works

Job seekers know their CVs are weak. They just do not know how — and they do not have time to fix it for every application. Candid is an agent that tailors and improves CVs, fast. Here is what building it taught us about ADLC.

The process was not linear — and that was fine

PoC was Conceptualize. Then came Build. Then integration — and integration was where quality and extensibility jumped. New tools and features became easy to add. The six phases were all present. They just did not happen in the order on the previous slide.

Roles interweave in practice

Prompt Engineer, HITL Designer, Blueprint Architect — one or two people, different hats at different moments. That is not a problem. That is how early-stage agent projects work, and ADLC still gives you the map.

Evaluation came too late — that is the lesson

CV Judges — the evaluation layer — should have been built during the PoC, not after. We knew what good looked like in our heads. We should have written it down as a dataset from day one.

HITL was not a gate — it was the whole experience

Every step of the CV process involved the user. Designing that rhythm — through CopilotKit and AG-UI — was as important as building the agent. That is what made Candid feel like a tool and not a black box.
